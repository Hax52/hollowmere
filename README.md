# The Bell Beneath Hollowmere

A short solo dark-folklore adventure with D&D-style rules, played as a scroll on your phone. Pick a class, make choices, roll a d20 for risky ones, fight turn by turn, and find one of five endings.

**Play:** https://hax52.github.io/hollowmere/

## AI storyteller (optional)

With a Gemini API key, you can type your own actions in any scene or fight, talk freely to the people you meet, and run into short random interludes. Gemini narrates and judges; the game rolls every die and caps every reward.

1. Get a free key at [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/apikey).
2. In the game, tap **Set it up** on the title screen (or **Sheet → AI settings** during play).
3. Paste the key and tap **Save and test**.

Your key is saved only in your own browser and sent only to Google's Gemini API. Never share your key or paste it into this repository.

Without a key the game plays fully with its scripted choices.

## Make it yours

- **Appearance** (title screen, or Sheet → Appearance): pick a scroll (Vellum, Bone, Candlelit, Lakewater, Peat, or follow your device), an ink colour, the lettering, and the text size, or turn off animations.
- **Your place in the tale** (step 2 of character creation): choose where you come from, each with a small perk, or write your own past. Pick a keepsake: once between rests it lets you reroll a failed check.
- **The world** (with the AI storyteller): set the tone, add up to three things that are true in Hollowmere, and name someone you know there. Change these mid-tale from Sheet → Shape the world.

## Tips

- On a phone, use your browser's **Add to Home Screen** to open it like an app.
- Your progress saves automatically in your browser.
