# The Bell Beneath Hollowmere

A short solo dark-folklore adventure with D&D-style rules, played as a scroll on your phone. Build a hero, make choices, roll a d20 for risky ones, fight turn by turn, and find one of five endings.

**Play:** https://hax52.github.io/hollowmere/

## Build your hero

Character creation is a checklist. Tap any step, or tap **Begin now** at any point.

1. **World**: the look of the scroll, and (with the AI storyteller) the setting, tone and a few truths of your own.
2. **Who**: name, pronouns, people (Human, Elf, Dwarf, Halfling, Orc-blooded, Tide-touched, or one you invent), how you look, temperament, and a portrait.
3. **Calling**: Warden, Cutpurse, Hedge Mage, Lantern Priest, or your own: pick its weapon, armour, toughness, two gifts and two saving throws.
4. **Abilities**: standard scores, roll 4d6, or 27-point buy. Tap two scores to swap them.
5. **Past**: where you come from, and a keepsake that lets you reroll a failed check once between rests.
6. **Skills**: choose your trained skills; Cutpurses also mark two for Expertise.
7. **Pack**: two things for the road (lamp oil, smoke, a net, caltrops, blessed salt, holy water or a draught).

## Fighting

Each turn you have an **action**, a **bonus action** and a **move**. Foes are either **Close** (they can hit you) or **Apart**, and each shows what it means to do next.

- **Fight**: strike, aim for legs, arms or eyes, shove, grapple, disarm.
- **Gifts**: spells and class abilities.
- **Move**: close in, fall back (close foes get a free swing), disengage, dodge, hide, flee, break free.
- **Words**: study a foe to learn its weaknesses, frighten it, talk it down, or speak a name you have learned.
- **Pack**: draughts, holy water, lamp oil, nets, smoke, caltrops, salt.
- **Here**: the place itself: lanterns, loose ceilings, doorways, the deep water.

Damage has types (fire, radiant, bludgeoning…), and foes can be weak to some and shrug off others. Conditions such as prone, held, blinded, burning and frightened all change the odds.

## Portraits and the narrator

- **Portraits** (title screen, Sheet → Portraits, or tap a face): upload a picture for your hero and for everyone you meet. Pictures stay in your browser.
- **Narrator** (the speaker button at the top): reads the tale aloud with your device's voice. Tap any passage to hear it again from there.

## AI storyteller (optional)

With a Gemini API key you can also:

- type your own actions in any scene, and improvise moves in a fight;
- meet short random interludes;
- **retell the whole tale in another setting** (a frozen fjord, a desert salt lake, a clockwork city, an ocean moon, a bayou, or one you describe);
- **paint portraits** with Gemini;
- hear the tale read by a **Gemini voice**.

Gemini narrates and judges; the game rolls every die and caps every reward.

1. Get a free key at [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/apikey).
2. In the game, tap **AI storyteller** on the title screen (or **Sheet → AI settings** during play).
3. Paste the key and tap **Save and test**.

Your key is saved only in your own browser and sent only to Google's Gemini API. Never share your key or paste it into this repository. Each retold scene, portrait and spoken passage is a request on your key.

Without a key the game plays fully with its scripted choices.

## Tips

- On a phone, use your browser's **Add to Home Screen** to open it like an app.
- Your progress saves automatically in your browser. Tales started in an older version carry on.
